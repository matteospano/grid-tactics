📖 Tactical Rulebook

1. Roster & Base Stats

Each player commands 5 cards: Fire, Water, Earth, Air, and Cloud. All cards start with 100 HP, 55 ATK, and 20 Base DEF.

2. Snake Draft Turn Order

Cards act in a strict Snake Order based on their slot number (#1 to #5):
You #1 → Bot #1 → Bot #2 → You #2 → You #3 → Bot #3...
When a card dies, it is skipped in the queue and your next surviving card steps up.

3. Move + Global Attack

On a card's turn, it moves 1 orthogonal step (or stays still) AND launches 1 global ranged attack at any enemy card.

4. Front-Line Cover (Obstruction)

Solid cards (Fire, Water, Earth) intercept attacks aimed at cards directly behind them in the same column. Air & Cloud are transparent—shots pass right through them unless targeted directly.

5. Adjacency Defense & Badges

Orthogonal neighbors modify your 20 Base Defense based on the incoming attacker's element:
• Strong Counter Ally: +30% (+6 DEF)
• Neutral / Same Ally: +10% (+2 DEF)
• Weak / Clashing Ally: -15% (-3 DEF)
On each card, the shield number shows its neutral defense, and small element badges show which specific attack types have extra (+6) or reduced (-3) protection!

6. Jolly Elemental Promotion

When your Fire, Water, or Earth card dies, at the start of your next turn you may permanently promote your neutral Air or Cloud card into that fallen element (gaining its strengths, weaknesses, and solid body-blocking).
