📜 Teutoburg: Tactical Field Manual

1. Two-Step Blind Simultaneous WEGO Rounds

During Tactical Pause, click any of your units in any order to plot movement paths and attack targets. Click Confirm Orders to reveal the Bot's orders on the field, then click Execute Battle to watch quarter-step movement and simultaneous clashes resolve!

2. Continuous Collisions, Traces & Focused Opportunity

  • Mid-Path Head-On Collisions: Units advance in continuous quarter-steps. When two charging armies cross paths, they halt at their fractional meeting point!
  • Focused Spearman Opportunity: Passing adjacent to enemy Spearmen triggers an automatic Opportunity Thrust ONLY if the Spearmen are holding position or aiming at your path—not if they are focused fighting elsewhere.
  • Multi-Tile Volley Traces: Aiming where an enemy moves scores grazing hits across every intercepted trace tile: 55% damage (1-tile walk), 40% (2-tile run), and 25% for galloping Cavalry (Archers only).
  • Simultaneous Clash Clustering: All combat resolves from a simultaneous snapshot with zero index priority—outnumbered squads always inflict their counter-casualties before falling.

3. Unit Classes & Catapult Reload

  • ⚔️ Legionaries & 🔱 Spearmen (1,000 men, Move 1–2): Directional Shield Wall (+35% DEF stationary, +20% 1-tile advance, +5% 2-tile march, 0%/-5% retreat). Spearmen deal +60% vs Cavalry and cannot be split.
  • 🐎 Cavalry (400 riders, Move 1–5): 4–5 tile charges in open terrain split non-spear infantry into two halves!
  • 🏹 Archers (600 men, Move 1–2, Range 6 / 4 in Woods): Deterministic spread (100% close, 70/15/15 mid, 60/10 cross long). Woods give +40% arrow DEF & block Cavalry charges.
  • ☄️ Catapults (20 engines, Stationary, Range 4–18): Fires a 5-tile cross AoE, then spends 1 Round Reloading (⏳). Deals 100% vs stationary, 65% vs 1-tile, 35% vs 2-tile, 0% vs galloping Cavalry, and max ~7–9 losses in Catapult duels.
