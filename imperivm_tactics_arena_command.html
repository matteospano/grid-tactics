📜 Imperivm Tactics: Field Manual

1. Simultaneous Turn Resolution ("Pause & Execute")

During the Planning Phase, you and the Bot alternate in Snake Order issuing movement paths and attack targets. Nothing happens until all 16 orders are locked in—then time unfreezes and all troops move, charge, and fire simultaneously!

2. Unit Classes & Formations

  • ⚔️ Legionaries (1,000 men, Move 1–2): Directional Shield Wall! Staying still gives +35% DEF; moving 1 tile forward/sideways keeps +20% DEF; 2 tiles forward/sideways keeps +5% DEF; retreating 1–2 tiles drops to 0% / -5% DEF.
  • 🔱 Spearmen (1,000 men, Move 1–2): Same directional Shield Wall plus +60% Bonus Damage vs Cavalry and immune to Cavalry formation splitting. Step forward 1–2 tiles to choke enemy Cavalry charge distance!
  • 🐎 Cavalry (400 riders, Move 1–5): Devastating momentum! Charging 4–5 tiles in open terrain smashes non-spear infantry—splitting a 1,000-man squad into two 450-man halves on adjacent flank tiles before melee! Reduced impact vs other Cavalry; +50% vs Catapults.
  • 🏹 Archers (600 men, Move 1–2, Range 6): Predictable arrow volley spread: 100% accuracy at 1–2 tiles; 70% center + 15% front/back at 3–4 tiles; 60% center + 10% cross at 5–6 tiles. Inside 🌲 Woods, gain +40% arrow defense & block Cavalry charges, but range drops to 4 tiles.
  • ☄️ Catapults (20 engines, Stationary, Range 4–18): Immobile long-range siege! Fires a 5-tile cross AoE (70% center / 7.5% arms at 4–9 tiles; 40% center / 15% arms at 10–18 tiles). Deals 100% damage to stationary targets, 50% to marching units (1–2 tiles), and 0% (Miss) to galloping Cavalry (3–5 tiles).

3. Voluntary Squad Splitting

Any multi-man squad can split once per battle during its Planning turn into two independent halves that share the same Snake Queue slot—allowing you to leave half in the woods while flanking with the rest, or re-merge them later by moving one onto the other.
