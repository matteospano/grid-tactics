📜 Teutoburg: Tactical Field Manual

1. Blind Simultaneous WEGO Rounds

During Tactical Pause, click any of your units in any order to plot movement paths and attack targets. Neither side sees the other's orders until you click Execute Battle Round—then both armies march, collide, and fire simultaneously!

2. Tick-by-Tick Collisions, Traces & Grazing

  • Mid-Path Head-On Collisions: Units advance step-by-step. If two charging armies cross paths, they halt at their meeting point! If Cavalry meets advancing Legionaries head-on, it still splits them, but both sides trade heavy casualties.
  • Spearman Zone of Control: Passing orthogonally adjacent to enemy Spearmen triggers an automatic Opportunity Thrust.
  • Movement Traces (Grazing): Aiming where an enemy just left hits their trailing ranks: 55% damage if they walked 1 tile, 40% if they ran 2 tiles, and 25% for galloping Cavalry (Archers only).
  • Predictive Melee Aim: Legionaries, Spearmen, and Cavalry can target empty adjacent tiles around their destination to intercept incoming enemies!

3. Unit Classes & Catapult Reload

  • ⚔️ Legionaries & 🔱 Spearmen (1,000 men, Move 1–2): Directional Shield Wall (+35% DEF stationary, +20% 1-tile advance, +5% 2-tile march, 0%/-5% retreat). Spearmen deal +60% vs Cavalry and cannot be split.
  • 🐎 Cavalry (400 riders, Move 1–5): 4–5 tile charges in open terrain split non-spear infantry into two halves!
  • 🏹 Archers (600 men, Move 1–2, Range 6 / 4 in Woods): Deterministic spread (100% close, 70/15/15 mid, 60/10 cross long). Woods give +40% arrow DEF & block Cavalry charges.
  • ☄️ Catapults (20 engines, Stationary, Range 4–18): Fires a 5-tile cross AoE, then spends 1 Round Reloading (⏳). Deals 100% vs stationary, 65% vs 1-tile, 35% vs 2-tile, 0% vs galloping Cavalry, and max ~7–8 losses in Catapult duels.
