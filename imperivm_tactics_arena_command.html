📖 Teutoburg Field Manual

1. Simultaneous WEGO & Quarter-Step Clashes

Both sides plot orders during Tactical Pause (with secret handoff in 1 vs 1 mode). After confirming orders, trajectories reveal before you click Execute Battle. Movement resolves in smooth quarter-steps so charging armies collide mid-path and trade mutual casualties simultaneously.

2. Woods (🌲) & Terrain

Units inside 🌲 take -40% arrow damage and are immune to Cavalry charge-splits. Archers firing from 🌲 have their max range reduced from 6 to 4.

3. Legionaries & Spearmen Stances

Stationary squads gain +35% DEF. Advancing/sideways 1 tile keeps +20% DEF; 2 tiles keeps +5% DEF. Retreating 1–2 tiles drops to 0% / -5% DEF. Spearmen counter Cavalry charges and can intercept passing enemies when holding or aiming at their path.

4. Cavalry Charge & Formation Split

Cavalry moves 1–5 tiles. Charging 4–5 tiles into non-spears in open terrain splits them into two sub-squads that can later re-merge or act independently (you can also voluntarily split multi-man squads once).

5. Volley Spread, Traces & Stationary Catapults

Longer ranged shots spread into adjacent tiles (hitting friendly or enemy units in the splash zone, including 55% / 40% / 25% grazing damage on movement traces). Catapults stay anchored in your zone (range 4–18, 5-tile cross AoE), reload every other round, and deal capped counter-battery losses in catapult duels.
