📜 Teutoburg Tactical Field Manual

1. Blind Simultaneous WEGO & Quarter-Step Physics: Both commanders issue orders to all living units simultaneously. Clicking Confirm Orders reveals the enemy's planned arrows before executing 16 fractional quarter-steps where opposing forces collide head-on at exact meeting squares.

2. Simultaneous Clash Clustering: All combat casualties in a clash are computed from simultaneous pre-clash strength—outnumbered units always deal their counter-casualties before falling.

3. Directional Shield Wall (Legionaries & Spearmen):
• Stationary (0 tiles): +35% DEF (Spearmen also get +50% vs Cavalry).
• 1 tile forward/sideways: +20% DEF | 2 tiles forward/sideways: +5% DEF.
• 1 tile retreat: 0% DEF | 2 tiles retreat: -5% DEF.
• Spearman Opportunity Thrust: Spearmen holding position or aiming along a passing enemy's trace strike units passing adjacent to them.

4. Cavalry Charge & Formation Splitting: Moves 1–5 tiles. Charging 4–5 tiles in open terrain splits non-spear squads into two halves on adjacent squares. You may also voluntarily Split any unified multi-man squad, or Merge two orthogonally adjacent halves of the same split unit back into a single unified squad before moving.

5. Deterministic Volley Dispersion & Speed-Scaled Grazing:
• Archers (Range 6 open, 4 in Woods): 1–2 tiles = 100% center; 3–4 tiles = 70% center + 15% front/back; 5–6 tiles = 60% center + 10% orthogonal cross.
• Catapults (Stationary, Range 4–18, 1-Round Reload): 4–9 tiles = 70% center + 7.5% cross; 10–18 tiles = 40% center + 15% cross. Hits 100% vs stationary, 65% vs 1-tile walk, 35% vs 2-tile sprint, 0% vs galloping Cavalry. Counter-battery duels deal at most ~8 engines per volley.
• Movement Traces: Firing onto a square an enemy vacated deals 55% grazing (if they walked 1 tile), 40% (if they ran 2 tiles), and 25% (Archers vs Cavalry).
